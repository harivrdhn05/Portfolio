Contribution-Aware Trust Mechanism for Swarm Learning

On-going research

A multi-factor trust score for swarm learning. It measures how each participant’s update contributes to the global model, then combines that with validation, update behaviour, similarity, and historical evidence. The score reduces the influence of poisoning participants while protecting honest ones whose data is heterogeneous.

  • PyTorch
